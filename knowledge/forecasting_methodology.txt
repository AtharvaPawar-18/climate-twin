ClimateTwin short-term forecasting uses Random Forest regression models.

The forecasting features include recent environmental observations, lagged measurements, rolling averages, and time-based variables.

The current target variables are temperature, humidity, heat index, and wind speed.

The forecasting data is resampled to a 15-minute interval.

Four 15-minute steps correspond to a one-hour forecast.

Eight 15-minute steps correspond to a two-hour forecast.

Twelve 15-minute steps correspond to a three-hour forecast.

The forecast models use a temporal train-test split rather than randomly shuffling observations.

Current prototype validation results are:

One hour:
Temperature R2 = 0.9416
Humidity R2 = 0.9339
Heat index R2 = 0.9401
Wind speed R2 = 0.2004

Two hours:
Temperature R2 = 0.8958
Humidity R2 = 0.8316
Heat index R2 = 0.8994
Wind speed R2 = 0.1277

Three hours:
Temperature R2 = 0.8616
Humidity R2 = 0.8038
Heat index R2 = 0.8600
Wind speed R2 = 0.1040

Forecast accuracy decreases as the prediction horizon increases.

Wind speed currently has substantially lower validation performance than temperature, humidity, and heat index. Wind predictions should therefore be interpreted cautiously.

The current forecasting results are prototype validation results on the available dataset and do not establish general weather forecasting accuracy.

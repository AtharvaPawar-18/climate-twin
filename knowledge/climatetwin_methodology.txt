ClimateTwin AI is designed as an environmental intelligence system that converts environmental observations into insight, prediction, explanation, and recommended action.

The system currently contains four major analytical layers.

The first layer is environmental monitoring, which presents current and historical Conduit observations.

The second layer is anomaly detection. ClimateTwin currently uses an Isolation Forest model to identify observations that differ from the learned normal pattern.

The prototype anomaly model uses temperature, pressure, humidity, radiation, wind, heat index, wet-bulb temperature, and wet-bulb globe temperature features.

The anomaly model uses a contamination configuration of 0.05. Therefore the observed anomaly proportion should not be interpreted as the natural frequency of real-world hazards.

The third layer is environmental intelligence. Prototype rules transform detected anomalies into event categories, priority levels, signals, and recommended actions.

These labels are prototype analytical outputs and should not be interpreted as official meteorological hazard classifications without additional validation.

The fourth layer is short-term forecasting. ClimateTwin predicts environmental variables at one-hour, two-hour, and three-hour horizons.

ClimateTwin is intended to support interpretation and decision-making from environmental data. It does not claim long-term climate forecasting or definitive prediction of floods, droughts, storms, or other hazards from the current prototype dataset.

ClimateTwin AI uses environmental observations obtained from the JKUAT Conduit data system.

The current prototype dataset contains environmental sensor observations collected approximately every 15 minutes from a single station.

The dataset includes timestamp, rainfall-related fields, temperature measurements, pressure, humidity, visible light, infrared radiation, ultraviolet radiation, wind speed, wind direction, wind gust, heat index, wet-bulb temperature, and wet-bulb globe temperature.

Important temperature-related fields include temp_bmx, temp_mcp, and temp_sht.

humidity_sht represents relative humidity.

wind_spd represents wind speed.

wind_dir represents wind direction.

wind_gust represents wind gust measurement.

heat_idx represents heat index.

wet_bulb_temp represents wet-bulb temperature.

wet_bulb_globe_temp represents wet-bulb globe temperature.

The current prototype dataset does not contain latitude and longitude coordinates.

The current ClimateTwin prototype uses Conduit observations as the underlying environmental data source.
